1. **Welcome / introduction**

2. **Founder story (Rubik's Cube)**

   > Most people hear "we buy businesses" and assume there's only one kind of deal on the table. 4. **Educational: what "creative financing" actually means**
   > "Creative financing" gets thrown around a lot in the small business world, and it's simpler than it sounds. It just means the deal doesn't have to be 100% cash at closing — seller financing, earn-outs, and structured partnerships all exist so a transition can work for both sides, not just whoever has the most capital on hand. ## Settings & Moderation Checklist

Confirmed against the live page (Aug 25, 2026):

- **Restrict who can post on your Page** (Settings → Page and tagging → "Who can post on your Page?"): currently **Everyone** — change to admins-only so strangers can't post directly to the Page timeline. Comments on your own posts are a separate setting and unaffected by this.
- **Hidden Words / profanity filter** (Meta Business Suite → Settings, under content moderation): turn on the built-in filter for common offensive language, and add a custom hidden-words list — spam phrases ("DM me," "check my bio," crypto/investment spam terms), plus anything else specific to the space. Hidden comments don't get deleted, just hidden from public view, so nothing is lost if it's a false positive.
- **Reviews/recommendations** (Settings → Page and tagging → "Allow others to view and leave reviews"): currently ON. With zero activity on the page, one bad-faith review would be very visible with nothing to balance it. Consider turning this off until there's more going on, then re-enabling later.
- **Page publishing/visibility:** confirm the Page is set to unpublished/unlisted while seeding content, then flip it to published when everything above is ready — avoids anyone stumbling onto an empty page mid-setup.
- **Messenger:** turn on an instant reply / away message so nobody who messages the Page gets left on read for days; message requests are filtered by default, which helps with spam DMs.
- **Admin access & backups:** make sure the Page is tied to Kyle's business email/account with 2FA turned on. If a trusted second person is ever added, use Business Suite's People/roles (not legacy Page roles) and give the minimum access needed.
- **Page Transparency:** nothing to do here yet — it's new, so there's no history to worry about, but worth knowing it's public (creation date, admin location country, any past name changes).
- **Meta Verified (optional):** a paid option worth considering later for credibility once the page has some real activity — not urgent right now.
