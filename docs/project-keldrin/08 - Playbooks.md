# 08 - Playbooks

## Purpose

Reusable frameworks for the acquisition process itself: seller discovery, sourcing, diligence, deal structuring, negotiation, and post-close operations. Distinct from [[05 - Systems & SOPs]], which covers internal operating/admin processes rather than deal methodology.

## Guiding Approach

Lead with listening, not "we buy businesses." Understand the owner's real constraint before prescribing a transaction (see [[00 - Project Keldrin Overview]] guardrails and [[01 - Brand DNA]]).

## Structures to Draw On

Seller financing, subject-to arrangements, partnerships, management agreements, master leases, equity splits, referrals — see [[04 - Deals]] for how these map to the emerging buy box.

## Open Areas (no formal playbooks written yet)

- Seller discovery / first-conversation framework
- Sourcing channels and outreach approach
- Diligence checklist by deal type
- Negotiation approach for creative-finance structures
- Post-close operations handoff
